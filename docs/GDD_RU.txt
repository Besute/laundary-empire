LAUNDRY EMPIRE — GAME DESIGN DOCUMENT (GDD)
Single source of truth for AI-assisted development in Roblox Studio
Version 1.0 | November 12, 2026 | Luau

1. PROJECT VISION

Laundry Empire is a multiplayer Roblox Simulator + Tycoon + Management game. The player starts in a small, abandoned laundromat near a shopping mall. They accept clothes from NPC customers, wash and dry them, and hand back completed orders. Then they improve equipment and interiors, hire employees, buy nearby buildings, build parking and additional floors, run advertising, protect the business at night, and grow one laundromat into an empire.

The core fantasy: “I started in a dirty, empty room and washed the first clothes myself. Now I own a beautiful network of laundromats, employees serve customers, cars park outside my branches, and my business helps the district grow.”

Design pillars:
- A clear, interactive customer-service loop.
- Visible business growth and building customization.
- A living city with NPCs, cars, shops, and changing time of day.
- Meaningful management of money, rating, staff, and expenses.
- Manageable risks: queues, dirt, breakdowns, and theft.
- Long-term progress through property, floors, branches, and Rebirth.

Visual style: a full 3D Roblox world, not an isometric game; blocky Roblox avatars; low-poly environments; pastel colors with turquoise accents; freely explorable interiors; and a clear, adaptive UI.

2. APPROVED DECISIONS

Laundry loading — MENU: The player interacts with a basket or machine, chooses item types and quantities in a UI, and confirms loading. The character automatically plays a loading animation while clothing models visibly move into the drum. Do not require the player to drag every clothing model manually.

Camera — HYBRID: Use the normal Roblox third-person camera during gameplay and a separate top-down or angled view in build mode. Restore the normal camera when build mode ends.

Employees — GRADUAL: The player does the work at first. Roles unlock over time, and each role automates a distinct operation. Employees have wages, speed, level, work area, and an assigned task.

Also approved:
- The player chooses a business name and one of three cosmetic facades: Modern, Classic, or Colorful.
- The starting laundromat is dirty, empty, worn, and dimly lit.
- Opening the business requires a washer, dryer, and order intake counter.
- The Laundry menu controls the business name, open/closed status, and schedule.
- NPC customers form a queue, lose patience, and may leave.
- The player may accept or politely decline an order.
- Total unfinished-order capacity is limited by washer capacity.
- Orders pass through washing, drying, packing, and pickup.
- The game includes a shop, employees, rating, dirt, breakdowns, taxes, advertising, parking, thieves, and police.
- The player can buy nearby buildings and expand horizontally and vertically.
- Rebirth and new districts are late-game features.

3. CORE GAMEPLAY LOOP

Improve the building, equipment, or advertising → open the laundromat → attract pedestrians and drivers → accept orders → load items through the menu → choose detergent and mode → wash → dry → pack → hand over the order → receive money, XP, and a review → maintain cleanliness and equipment → reinvest the profit.

A short session includes several orders, checking queues and machines, cleaning, and restocking supplies. A medium session may include hiring a worker, improving the interior, buying equipment, or running an ad campaign. A long session may include expanding the premises, buying an adjacent property, joining an auction, or handling a nighttime event.

The MVP has no offline production or offline income. Orders and machine cycles run only during an active server session.

4. START AND TUTORIAL

The player spawns near a shopping mall and sees an abandoned laundromat. They enter a name, choose a facade, and go inside. The interior has old wallpaper, a dirty floor, weak lighting, empty space, and old windows.

Tutorial steps:
- T1: Open the build menu.
- T2: Buy and place a washer.
- T3: Buy and place a dryer.
- T4: Buy and place the counter.
- T5: Open the Laundry menu.
- T6: Check the name and schedule.
- T7: Open the business.
- T8: Accept the first order.
- T9: Process it through washing and drying.
- T10: Hand over the order and receive the reward.

The tutorial is contextual, highlights the needed object, and does not block controls for long. It is complete when the player hands over their first order without assistance.

5. DOWNTOWN

The starting district contains a shopping mall, main street, two-lane road, sidewalks, crosswalks, an intersection, streetlights, trees, benches, a supply shop, an advertising agency, ad locations, the starter laundromat, and abandoned buildings on both sides.

Abandoned buildings have broken windows, cracks, peeling paint, trash, old signs, and overgrown lots. The player can buy and renovate them, then assign a use: laundromat, storage, parking, office, or extra area.

For the MVP, individual property progression is enough. A shared district rating is a later feature.

NPC types: background pedestrians, potential customers, customers with orders, drivers, and later VIP customers and corporate representatives. Cars follow predefined waypoints; do not use expensive physics for large numbers of vehicles.
6. PROPERTY AND BUILDING

Property types:
- Small units: shops, cafes, and small commercial spaces.
- Medium buildings: two-story units, warehouses, and offices.
- Large lots: industrial buildings, parking areas, and logistics centers.

Property states: Available → ReservedForPurchase → Owned → UnderRenovation → Operational. Additional states: Locked and Disabled.

The server validates funds, availability, and ownership during a purchase. A player cannot buy another player’s property, purchase the same property twice, or lose money when a purchase fails.

Build mode includes:
- A placement grid and transparent preview.
- Green for a valid placement and red for an invalid placement.
- Rotate, confirm, and cancel controls.
- Lot-boundary and overlap checks.
- Server validation of coordinates and price.
- Saving of position, rotation, and floor.

Categories: equipment; intake; storage; interior; lighting; sanitation; security; walls, windows, doors, stairs, floors, and roofs; parking lines and barriers.

Vertical expansion requires floors, stairs, walls, and windows. Elevators and connecting separate buildings are post-MVP. Expensive decor must not be required for a good rating.

7. NPC CUSTOMERS AND STATES

Customer data: CustomerId, CustomerType, AppearanceId, PreferredService, Patience, Mood, ChosenBusinessId, CurrentState, OrderId, ArrivalTime, ReturnTime, TargetPosition.

States:
Spawned — created.
Walking — following a route.
Browsing — looking around the street.
Interested — noticed a sign.
ApproachingBusiness — walking to a laundromat.
Entering — entering the building.
JoiningQueue — taking a place in the queue.
Waiting — waiting.
Impatient — becoming irritated.
Leaving — leaving.
OrderAccepted — order accepted; customer exits the premises.
AwayWithOrder — waiting for the order to be ready.
Returning — coming back.
WaitingForPickup — waiting for handoff.
Satisfied — received the clothes and is happy.
Dissatisfied — left a negative review.
Completed — interaction finished.
Despawned — model removed.

A customer chooses a laundromat based on attractiveness, reputation, advertising, distance, cleanliness, whether it is open, queue length, and parking.

Example formula:
ChoiceScore = 0.25 Attractiveness + 0.25 Reputation + 0.15 Advertising + 0.15 Accessibility + 0.10 Cleanliness + 0.10 ParkingBonus − DistancePenalty

Weights are configurable; normalize probabilities across available laundromats.

Limit active NPCs. Background NPCs may use simplified behavior. Stuck NPCs need a timeout and a path retry.

8. ORDERS, QUEUE, AND PATIENCE

Order card: customer, item list, quantity, service, payment, estimated time, requirements, Accept button, and Decline button.

Order states: Offered, Accepted, WaitingForWash, Washing, WaitingForDryer, Drying, Finishing, ReadyForPickup, Completed, Failed, Cancelled. OrderService controls transitions. An order cannot jump directly from Offered to Completed.

Queue rules:
- NPCs queue in arrival order.
- Each customer has Patience and WaitTime.
- Patience gradually decreases.
- Mood states: calm, worried, annoyed, leaving.
- Show patience above the NPC or in the UI.
- At zero patience, the NPC leaves and creates a negative-review event.
- The queue has a limit; new visitors may pass by when it is full.

A polite decline before accepting an order is not an order failure and should not carry the same penalty as ignoring a customer. Show the player the reason for declining.

After accepting an order, the customer may leave and return later. The order is its own entity and does not disappear with the NPC.

Orders have EstimatedReadyAt and CustomerReturnAt. If the customer returns before the order is ready, they wait for extra time. Once pickup patience expires, the player may receive a payment penalty and a negative review. Show the player an estimated time.

Capacity rules:
- Each item has CapacityUnits.
- Each washer has CapacityUnits.
- UnfinishedUnits is the total units in all accepted orders that have not been handed over.
- AvailableUnits is the total available capacity of working washers minus UnfinishedUnits.
- Accept an order only when UnfinishedUnits + NewOrderUnits <= TotalAvailableUnits.
- The server performs this check.
- For the MVP, count every unfinished order, including items in baskets, washers, and dryers.
- A breakdown does not remove an order that was already accepted.

9. WASHING, DRYING, AND PICKUP

Menu-based loading is the approved method. The player interacts with a basket or machine, chooses item types and quantities, and presses Load. The server validates machine ownership, state, item availability, capacity, compatibility, and that the items have not already been loaded. The character plays an animation while clothing models visibly move into the drum.

Item types: T-shirts, jeans, towels, shirts, bed linen, and delicate items.

Products: regular detergent, professional detergent, delicate-fabric detergent, fabric softener, bleach, stain remover, and fragrance. Each product has ItemId, price, stock, quality bonus, compatible fabrics, and an unlock level. Consumables are used up; a required cycle cannot start without the required product.

Wash modes:
- Quick — shorter cycle.
- Normal — balance between time and quality.
- Delicate — for sensitive fabrics.
- Intensive — for heavy soil.

Machine states: Idle, Loading, ReadyToStart, Washing, CycleComplete, Unloading, NeedsMaintenance, Broken, Repairing, Disabled.

After washing, the player selects items in the dryer menu. The character carries them with an animation. A dryer has capacity and a timer. A drying rack is cheaper, but slower and takes up space.

After drying, the player gathers and packs the order through a menu or interactive table. Its status becomes ReadyForPickup. When the customer returns, the player presses Hand Over. The server checks that the order is ready and grants payment and XP exactly once.

Quality depends on the correct mode and product, equipment condition, timeliness, and optional services. A small mistake must not destroy the entire order.

10. EQUIPMENT AND BREAKDOWNS

Machine parameters: MachineId, MachineType, Capacity, CycleDurationMultiplier, QualityMultiplier, Wear, Condition, Durability, CurrentOrderIds, CurrentCycleEndTime, OwnerId, PropertyId.

Wear increases after cycles and may depend on the selected mode. At a threshold, the machine warns that maintenance is required. Ignoring the warning increases the chance of a breakdown.

States: Working → NeedsMaintenance → Broken → Repairing → Working.

On breakdown, the cycle stops or enters an emergency state. Items and orders are preserved, and the player sees which orders were affected. The player can call a mechanic or move items to a free compatible machine. Repairs have a cost and duration. Preventive maintenance is cheaper than emergency repair.
11. RATING, REVIEWS, AND CLEANLINESS

Rating is 0–100 and includes:
- ServiceRating — service.
- CleanlinessRating — cleanliness.
- InteriorRating — interior.
- SpeedRating — speed.
- ReviewRating — reviews.

Starting weights: service 30%, cleanliness 20%, interior 15%, speed 20%, reviews 15%. All weights are configurable.

Positive review: the order was on time and good quality. Neutral review: ordinary experience. Negative review: waiting, lateness, poor quality, or dirt.

NPCs may leave paper, cups, packaging, shoe marks, and stains. The player cleans with a broom, mop, or vacuum. Bins help NPCs dispose of trash properly but may need to be emptied.

Create and remove dirt through events; do not recalculate it every frame. Long-lasting dirt lowers the rating. Decor includes plants, sofas, TVs, pictures, clocks, neon, lights, flooring, wallpaper, and windows. Limit the bonus from duplicate items.

12. ECONOMY, XP, AND PROGRESSION

Currencies: Cash is the main game currency. Gems are premium currency for cosmetics and optional conveniences.

Cash sources: completed orders, tips, parking fees (if paid), optional services, and later corporate orders and events. Expenses: equipment, consumables, property, repairs, wages, taxes, advertising, construction, decor, and parking.

XP is primarily awarded for completed orders. Cleaning, repairs, and events may grant extra XP, but repeated clicks must not grant infinite experience.

Levels unlock machines, products, decor, employees, advertising, expansion, and property types. Some unlocks also require money, rating, or achievements.

Charge taxes on a clear schedule, such as at the end of a game day. Show an estimate in advance. A new player must not unexpectedly lose their starting capital. If funds are insufficient, use a warning or limited debt instead of deleting the business.

Finance panel: balance, daily income, expenses, net profit, wages, purchases, repairs, taxes, advertising, and parking.

Starting playtest hypotheses:
- Starting Cash: 2,500–4,000.
- Basic washer: 700–1,000.
- Dryer: 500–800.
- Counter: 200–350.
- Regular detergent: 20–40.
- Cleaning tool: 100–250.
- Decor item: 50–150.
- Average order: 80–150.
- Starting queue: 2–3 NPCs.
- Full game day: 30–40 real minutes.
- Basic machine capacity: about 8 CapacityUnits.
All values are hypotheses stored in configuration.

Target feel: the first order is quick; the player sees their first money within minutes; the first upgrade is reachable without grinding; automation unlocks after the player learns the manual loop; expenses create choices without constant financial anxiety; and penalties have clear causes.

13. EMPLOYEES AND AUTOMATION

Roles:
- Cashier — queue, order intake, and handoff.
- Washer — load and start wash cycles.
- Dryer — move items to dryers.
- Packer — pack orders.
- Cleaner — clean.
- Mechanic — maintenance and repairs.
- SecurityGuard — respond to threats.
- Manager — later, manage areas or branches.

Parameters: EmployeeId, Role, Level, WorkSpeed, Salary, Efficiency, AssignedTask, WorkZone, ShiftStart, ShiftEnd, Status, Experience.

States: Available, Working, MovingToTask, PerformingTask, Waiting, OnBreak, Disabled, Fired.

Assign tasks through a server-managed queue. One indivisible order cannot be processed by two employees at the same time. If supplies or equipment are unavailable, the employee must not repeat an impossible task forever. Pay wages on a schedule. Keep manual controls available.

14. SHOP AND INVENTORY

Physical shop near the intersection:
- Cleaning products.
- Cleaning tools.
- Baskets, shelves, filters, and spare parts.
- Dispensers, steam generators, ironing stations, sorters, and packing machines.

Inventory stores ItemId, quantity, maximum volume, price, usage type, and unlock level. The server confirms purchases and consumption. A player cannot use more items than they own. Delivery to the laundromat is post-MVP.

15. ADVERTISING AND AUCTIONS

Ad types: flyers, small banner, large billboard, bus stop, bus, digital screen, online campaign, city-wide campaign.

Advertising raises the chance that customers choose the laundromat; it does not create customers from nowhere. Consider NPC traffic, whether the business is open, queue size, and capacity.

The advertising agency is a physical building on the map. The player chooses an ad slot and duration.

Auction flow: a slot opens; players bid; the server validates funds; funds are reserved; the winner receives the slot for the selected duration; then the slot is released. Refund losing bids. Do not take an active, paid campaign before it expires. A bid may be outbid only during an open auction.
16. DAY, NIGHT, THIEVES, AND POLICE

A game day lasts about 30–40 real minutes. The server synchronizes the time.

Periods: 06:00–09:00 morning; 09:00–15:00 daytime; 15:00–19:00 evening; 19:00–22:00 late customers; 22:00–02:00 night events; 02:00–06:00 low activity. A 24-hour laundromat may accept visitors at night.

Thief states: Spawned → Approaching → BreakingIn → Stealing/Destroying → Detected → Escaping or Captured → PoliceCalled → Arrested → Despawned.

Targets: steal a limited amount of cash from the register or damage equipment, furniture, a door, or a window.

Detection: visual detection or a camera, alarm, or guard signal. The notification shows the threat and its location.

Capture: a short QTE/hold meter. After capture, the player calls the police. Police arrive by route. If the thief escapes, the damage is limited and repairable. The player must never lose the entire business.

Protection: cameras, alarms, guards, a protected register, doors, locks, and lighting. Events are rare at early levels.

17. PARKING AND TRANSPORT

Parking sizes: small — 3 spaces; medium — 6; large — 12; multi-level — 25; VIP — 8. These counts are preliminary.

Space states: Free, Reserved, Occupied, Leaving, Disabled.

Flow: a driver NPC chooses a laundromat; reserves a specific space; drives to the entrance; parks; the passenger walks to the laundromat; after the visit, returns to the car; the car leaves; the space becomes free. Reservations are required so two cars cannot choose the same space.

Upgrades: painted lines, lighting, barrier gate, cameras, EV charging, VIP spaces, automatic payment. Pedestrian customers remain a full source of orders.

18. PROPERTY, EXPANSION, AND LATE GAME

The player buys nearby buildings, renovates them, and chooses their use. They can open another branch, warehouse, parking area, office, or extra space.

Horizontal expansion: new rooms, wash area, storage, staff room, waiting area, VIP lounge. Vertical expansion: floors, stairs, walls, windows, and equipment on new floors; elevators come later.

Example floor plan: first floor — intake and standard washing; second — machines and drying; third — VIP; fourth — office.

Late game: VIP customers, corporate orders from hotels and restaurants, linen delivery, industrial laundromat, branch network, managers, franchises, Suburb/Industrial District/Rich District/Harbor districts, Rebirth, and seasonal events.

Rebirth is voluntary: some regular progress resets, while cosmetic rewards and permanent bonuses remain. Do not implement Rebirth until the economy is stable.

19. INTERFACES

Persistent HUD: Cash, Gems after unlock, level/XP, rating, game time, notifications, and a compact order panel.

Context interaction: E on PC, a large button on mobile, and gamepad support.

Menus: Build, Business, Orders, Shop, Employees, Advertising, Security, Finance, Settings.

Business: name, facade, open/close, schedule, rating, traffic, statistics, warnings.
Order: customer, items, quantity, cost, deadline, requirements, accept/decline.
Machine: state, capacity, items, product, mode, timer, maintenance.
Build: categories, price, preview, rotate, confirm, cancel.

Notifications: new customer, order ready, customer returned, machine maintenance needed, detergent out of stock, thief detected, campaign ended, tax due soon.

The UI adapts to PC, phones, tablets, and gamepads. Color must not be the only way to communicate a state. Sound can be turned off.

20. MONETIZATION AND MULTIPLAYER

Monetization is primarily cosmetic: facades, signs, interior sets, employee skins, money effects, machine and car skins, VIP parking design, layout slots, and seasonal items.

Basic machines, detergent, and the core loop must not be paywalled. The server confirms purchases through MarketplaceService.

Multiplayer: each player has their own laundromat; money, orders, and property are individual; NPCs and events may be shared; auctions are competitive. A player cannot change another player’s objects or take another player’s orders. Visiting other laundromats and district goals are later features.
21. ROBLOX TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE

Use Roblox Luau. The server is authoritative for money, property, orders, and progression. The client handles UI, input, camera, previews, and visual effects. Shared types/configs go in ReplicatedStorage; server logic in ServerScriptService; server templates in ServerStorage; client controllers in StarterPlayerScripts; UI in StarterGui.

Recommended structure:

ReplicatedStorage/Shared/Config: EconomyConfig, MachineConfig, ItemConfig, OrderConfig, EmployeeConfig, EventConfig, BuildingConfig, NPCConfig.
ReplicatedStorage/Shared/Types: PlayerDataTypes, OrderTypes, MachineTypes, PropertyTypes.
ReplicatedStorage/Shared/Remotes: RequestPurchase, RequestBuild, RequestMoveObject, RequestAcceptOrder, RequestDeclineOrder, RequestLoadItems, RequestStartWash, RequestTransferItems, RequestStartDry, RequestCollectOrder, RequestSetBusinessHours, RequestToggleBusiness, RequestHireEmployee, RequestAssignEmployee, RequestClean, RequestRepair, RequestBid, NotifyClient.
ReplicatedStorage/Shared/Assets: shared assets.

ServerScriptService/Server/Services: PlayerDataService, PlotService, PropertyService, BusinessService, BuildingService, OrderService, MachineService, CustomerService, EmployeeService, EconomyService, InventoryService, RatingService, CleanlinessService, MaintenanceService, SecurityService, AdvertisingService, AuctionService, ParkingService, TrafficService, DayNightService, NotificationService, AnalyticsService.

ServerStorage/ServerAssets: NPCModels, VehicleModels, MachineModels, BuildingModules, EmployeeModels, EventAssets.

StarterPlayer/StarterPlayerScripts/Controllers: InteractionController, BuildingController, OrderController, MachineController, CameraController, NotificationController, AudioController.

StarterGui: MainHUD, BusinessMenu, BuildMenu, OrderMenu, MachineMenu, ShopMenu, EmployeeMenu, AdvertisingMenu, SecurityMenu, FinanceMenu, TutorialUI.

Workspace: City/Roads, Sidewalks, Intersections, PedestrianPaths, VehiclePaths, Properties, Shops, AdvertisingSlots; PlayerPlots; ActiveNPCs; ActiveVehicles; WorldEvents.

Service responsibilities:
- PlayerDataService — load/save, locking, migrations.
- PlotService — plots.

- BusinessService — name, hours, status.
- BuildingService — placement and floors.
- OrderService — order states, deadlines, rewards.

- EconomyService — money, XP, expenses, taxes.
- InventoryService — consumables.

- NotificationService — notifications.
- AnalyticsService — diagnostics.

RemoteEvents need a clear purpose, argument types, server-side validation, rate limits, and clear replies. Do not create one RemoteEvent per object. The server validates every distance, permission, and state.

22. SAVES AND DATA MODEL

Logical PlayerData: SchemaVersion, Cash, Gems, XP, Level, PrestigeLevel, Reputation, Business{Name, ExteriorStyle, IsOpen, OpeningTime, ClosingTime, Rating, ServiceRating, CleanlinessRating, InteriorRating, SpeedRating, ReviewRating}, Properties[], PlacedObjects[], Machines[], Inventory, Employees[], Orders[], Advertising[], Upgrades[], Statistics, TutorialProgress, LastSaveTimestamp.

Property: PropertyId, Type, Purchased, Floors, Condition, ExpansionData.
PlacedObject: ObjectId, ItemType, PropertyId, Position, Rotation, Floor, Durability, Condition.
Machine: MachineId, MachineType, Capacity, Condition, Wear, CurrentCycle.
Employee: EmployeeId, Role, Level, AssignedTask, WorkZone, Salary, Efficiency.
Order: OrderId, Items, Status, AcceptedAt, Deadline, Quality, Payment, CustomerReturnAt.

DataStore requirements:
- Use DataStoreService and pcall.
- Use UpdateAsync for safe updates.
- Lock profiles against simultaneous loading on multiple servers.
- Autosave at the configured interval.
- Save on PlayerRemoving and BindToClose.
- Save critical operations after purchases, handoff, and ownership changes.
- Do not save every visual tick.
- SchemaVersion and migrations are mandatory.
- Do not report a save as successful after an error.
- Retry with limits and backoff.
- Do not save temporary NPC models or effects.

On rejoin, restore unfinished orders and machine states. Never grant a reward twice. Offline timers do not complete washing cycles in the MVP.

Give each money operation and handoff a unique TransactionId/OrderId. A repeated request must not grant or charge currency twice.

23. SECURITY AND ANTI-EXPLOIT

Only the server changes money, XP, rating, and ownership. The client does not determine price, reward, plot owner, or purchase result. The server validates distance, permissions, object state, funds, inventory, argument types, and RemoteEvent frequency. The server confirms auction bids and MarketplaceService purchases. Client timers are not authoritative. Log important transactions.

24. PERFORMANCE

Limit NPCs and cars; use pooling; simplify distant NPCs; use centralized updates; enable StreamingEnabled for a large map; avoid excessive physical parts and dynamic lights; anchor static objects; use simple collision; avoid replicating unnecessary data; keep visual effects local when they do not affect gameplay results; test on PC and mobile devices.

26. TESTING AND MVP CRITERIA

Functional tests:
- A new player completes the tutorial.
- A returning player loads saved progress.
- A player cannot buy another player’s plot.
- A player cannot build outside the plot.
- A player cannot accept an order beyond capacity.
- A player cannot load items they do not own.
- A broken machine cannot start.
- An unfinished order cannot be handed over.
- Payment cannot be collected twice.
- A purchase cannot succeed without funds.
- Inventory cannot become negative.
- Two machines cannot reserve the same parking space.
- One ad slot cannot be won twice.
- An order does not disappear when its player leaves.

Failure handling:
- DataStore is unavailable.
- A player leaves during washing or repair.
- A server closes with active orders.
- A RemoteEvent is sent repeatedly.
- An NPC gets stuck.
- A car cannot find parking.
- An employee cannot perform a task.

Multiplayer:
- Players receive different plots.
- Players cannot change other players’ objects.
- NPCs are not duplicated without limit.
- Auctions handle simultaneous bids correctly.
- Time is synchronized.

The MVP is ready when a player creates a laundromat, places the three required objects, opens it, accepts an NPC order, loads items through the menu, chooses detergent and a mode, finishes washing and drying, packs and hands over the items, receives a reward, improves the business, and saves progress.

Not required for MVP: auctions, complex police, multiple districts, Rebirth, corporate orders, delivery, industrial factory, complex multi-floor systems, and offline income.
